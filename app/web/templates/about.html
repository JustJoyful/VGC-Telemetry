Unknowns

Unknown means the source did not establish a value. It does not mean zero. A confirmed empty held-item slot is also different from an unknown item.

  • Private build totals: unknown.
  • Missing moves, abilities, items and natures remain unknown.
  • Native Stat Points are unknown when the tournament does not publish them. Do not convert legacy EV units into Champions allocations.
  • Registered Mega capability does not prove activation in battle.
Freshness

Capture is when information was obtained. Publication is when it became available in a snapshot. Stale means the reported capture is older than 24 hours. Without a capture time, freshness is unknown. A retained query can refer to an older snapshot than the current status.

Reference data

Official M-B/M-C notices establish regulation intervals and eligible pools. Pinned community Pokémon Showdown data supplements mechanics, abilities, items and learnsets; it is not an official game patch export. Historical M-A and unknown intermediate patches remain unverified.

Reference data describes what is possible. Observed builds describe what a player publicly registered. Stat Points and Registered Mega capability retain their native Champions meaning.
